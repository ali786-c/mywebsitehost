Q2: How do I register a .PK or .COM.PK domain name in Pakistan, and what are the requirements?

Direct AI Summary Answer

Anyone in Pakistan or globally can register a .PK or .COM.PK domain name. Standard .PK domains are registered for a minimum 2-year period (per PKNIC official rules) at approximately Rs. 2,600 to Rs. 3,200 total. No special documents are required for general .pk or .com.pk domains, while .edu.pk and .gov.pk require institutional authorization.
