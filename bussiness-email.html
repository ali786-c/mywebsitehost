Frequently Asked Questions

Explore our FAQ to find quick answers to the most common questions about our products and services.

  • How will purchasing Business Email benefit me?

    Purchasing Business Email offers secure communication, professional features, collaboration tools, and ample storage, enhancing efficiency and establishing a credible and reliable online business presence.

  • Which Email Clients and protocols are supported?

    Our Business Email supports popular email clients like Outlook, Thunderbird, and mobile devices, using standard protocols such as IMAP, POP3, and SMTP for flexible and accessible communication.

  • How do I use my Webmail Interface?

    Access your webmail interface by logging into your email hosting account. Navigate through the user-friendly interface to manage emails, contacts, calendars, and settings effortlessly.

  • Which mobile phones can I access my mail from?

    You can access your mail from a variety of mobile phones, including iOS and Android devices. Simply configure your email account using the standard IMAP or POP3 settings.

  • What is the space provided per Email Account?

    The space provided per email account depends on the hosting plan. Generally, it ranges from 5GB to unlimited storage, ensuring ample space for your business email communications and data.

  • What ports do I need to use for Email Hosting?

    Use the following ports for Email Hosting: IMAP (Port 143 or 993 for secure SSL), POP3 (Port 110 or 995 for secure SSL), and SMTP (Port 25 or 587 for secure SSL).

  • Can I create mailing lists?

    Yes, you can create mailing lists with our Email Hosting. Utilize this feature to efficiently manage and send emails to specific groups or audiences for your business communication needs.

  • What is your SPAM policy?

    Our SPAM policy ensures a secure and clean email environment. Strict measures are taken to prevent, detect, and eliminate spam, maintaining a high-quality email service.
