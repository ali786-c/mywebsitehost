How to choose in five steps

  1. Start with your real need. One small website, blog or business site fits shared or WordPress hosting. Several websites, a busy store or full server control points toward cloud or VPS.
  2. Check the full price, not the headline. Confirm the renewal price, whether SSL is included, and whether backups, cPanel and migration are in the plan or added at checkout.
  3. Confirm local payment. Make sure you can pay in PKR with Easypaisa, JazzCash, Raast or bank transfer, without an international card or conversion fee.
  4. Check support before you need it. Note the support hours and contact options. ZedgeHost includes 24/7 support — test that you can actually reach a provider before your website depends on it.
  5. Know the way out. A 30-day money-back guarantee means you can try the service and leave if it does not fit. Read the guarantee terms before you pay, with any provider.
