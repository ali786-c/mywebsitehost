# Zedgehost External SEO Plan (Pakistan)

Yeh guide batata hai ke Zedgehost ke liye kaun se external platforms use karne hain, wahan kya karna hai, aur kis cheez se bachna hai. Maqsad relevant audience aur genuine brand mentions hasil karna hai; backlinks ki tadaad ya first-page ranking ki guarantee nahi.

## 30 din ka starter schedule

### Hafta 1: Brand profiles

- Official logo, business name, website, support email/phone aur service descriptions ek jagah standardize karein.
- LinkedIn aur Facebook company profiles complete karein.
- Google Business Profile/Bing Places ki eligibility check karein; eligible hon tabhi listing create ya claim karein.

### Hafta 2: Helpful content share karein

- Ek practical guide/video banayein: “Pakistan mein domain ko hosting se kaise connect karein” ya “WordPress site migrate karne ke steps”.
- Usi topic ko LinkedIn/Facebook par short summary ke saath share karein.
- Relevant Quora sawalon ka jawab dein; forum rules ke mutabiq Reddit par participate karein.

### Hafta 3: Real-world relationships

- 5 Pakistani web design agencies, freelancers ya tech publishers identify karein jinki audience Zedgehost ke customers se milti ho.
- Har contact ko personalized message bhejein: specific resource ya collaboration propose karein; guaranteed backlink ya exact-match anchor na maangein.
- Existing customers se feedback/case study ki permission maangein; review ke badle discount ya reward offer na karein.

### Hafta 4: Review aur repeat

- Search Console mein target pages ke clicks, impressions aur average position ko pichlay 28 din se compare karein.
- Referral traffic aur genuinely relevant new mentions note karein.
- Jo platform par real engagement aaye, aglay mahine usi par zyada waqt dein.

## Kya avoid karna hai

- “1000 backlinks”, paid dofollow links, Private Blog Networks (PBNs), automated comments/profile links, ya bulk directory packages.
- Har page ko same keyword wale exact-match anchor se link karwana.
- Fake reviews, fabricated customer logos/case studies, ya aisi business address details jo haqeeqi nahi.
- Quora, Reddit, Facebook groups ya forums mein repeated promotional links.
- Web 2.0 sites sirf backlinks banane ke liye banana.

Google ke mutabiq ranking manipulate karne ke maqsad se links banana link spam ho sakta hai. Sponsored/paid link placement ho to publisher se Google ke mutabiq `rel="sponsored"` ya `nofollow` qualification ka intizam karein.

## Monthly tracking sheet

Har mahine yeh record karein:

1. Search Console: clicks, impressions, CTR aur average position (target landing page ke hisaab se).
2. Referrals: platform/source, visits aur inquiry/sale agar analytics configured ho.
3. Mentions: site/brand ka naam, topic ki relevance, link hai ya nahi, aur contact/date.
4. Reviews: sirf asli customers ke voluntary reviews aur unka response status.

External SEO dheere build hota hai; results kuch hafton se kai mahino tak le sakte hain aur guaranteed nahi. Is schedule ko har mahine actual Search Console data ke mutabiq adjust karein.
