What actually affects the price?

Six things decide what you end up paying. Understanding them is what stops you from buying too little — or far too much.

1. The type of hosting

Shared hosting is the cheapest because many websites share one server and its cost. VPS hosting gives your website its own guaranteed CPU, RAM and storage, so it costs more. Cloud hosting sits above shared hosting and is built for websites that need room to grow. The type matters more than any other factor.

2. Resources: CPU, RAM and storage

A small blog and a busy online store do not need the same server. More CPU cores, more RAM and more SSD storage cost more — on VPS plans this is exactly what separates the Rs 1,699 plan (1 CPU core, 2 GB RAM, 40 GB SSD) from the Rs 4,999 plan (2 CPU cores, 8 GB RAM, 160 GB SSD). Buy for the traffic you realistically expect in the next year, not for traffic you hope to have one day.

3. SSL certificate

SSL (the padlock in the browser) is now essential — for security and for search rankings. Some providers charge extra for it every year. ZedgeHost includes a free SSL certificate with its hosting plans, so it is not an added cost here. Whenever you compare providers, check whether SSL is included or billed separately.

4. Backups and migration

Backups protect you when an update breaks something or a file is deleted by mistake. Website migration matters if you already have a website somewhere else and want to move it without downtime. ZedgeHost includes daily backups and free website migration, and both are worth checking with any provider before you buy.

5. Renewal price and contract length

With many providers, the price on the sales page is an introductory price and the renewal price is higher. Always confirm whether the price you see is the price you will pay again next year, and what happens if you pay monthly instead of yearly. Ask the question before you pay — with any provider, including us.

6. Support

When a website goes down at midnight, support is the product. ZedgeHost includes 24/7 expert support with its plans. Cheap hosting with no reachable support usually costs more in the end, in lost visitors and lost time.

What else will you pay for?

Budget for three things beyond the hosting plan itself:

  • A domain name. Your website’s address (like yourbusiness.com) is registered separately and renewed yearly. You can search and register a domain, including .pk domains, and point it at your hosting.
  • Email (optional). A business email address on your own domain looks more professional than a free mailbox. Check whether email accounts are included in your plan before buying a separate email service.
  • Optional extras. Things like premium security add-ons or a paid SSL upgrade are optional. Start without them; add them only when you have a clear reason.

One cost you should not face is a surprise at checkout: ZedgeHost bills in PKR and accepts Easypaisa, JazzCash, Raast and bank transfer, so there are no currency conversion surprises for Pakistani customers. See how ordering works before you buy.

How to choose in four steps

  1. Count your websites and visitors. One small website with modest traffic fits shared or WordPress hosting. Several websites, or one busy website, points toward cloud or VPS.
  2. Match resources to reality. Pick the smallest plan that honestly covers your needs today. Upgrading later is easy; paying for idle resources is not refunded time.
  3. Check what is included. SSL, backups, cPanel, migration and support should be in the price, not added at checkout. Compare the full list, not just the headline number.
  4. Confirm the renewal terms. Know what you will pay next year, and whether yearly billing saves you anything, before you enter your payment details.
