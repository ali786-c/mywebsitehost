Privacy Policy

Review the Privacy Policy of Zedgehost. Learn how we securely collect, use, and protect your personal and website data across all our hosting services.

1. Introduction to Zedgehost Privacy Policy

At Zedgehost ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is our top priority. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring that your web hosting, domain registration, and cloud services experience is secure. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit zedgehost.com or use our hosting services.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign up for Zedgehost web hosting or domain services, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing address.

Zedgehost uses the collected data to:

  • Send you important updates regarding server maintenance or Zedgehost promotional offers.

4. Data Security and Protection

Zedgehost employs industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption, DDoS protection, and regular server backups, to protect your personal and website data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data stored with Zedgehost. You can manage your account details via the Zedgehost Client Area. For any privacy-related queries, you can contact our support team.
